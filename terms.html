Text message terms

These terms apply to OpOrd billing texts, sent by OpOrd, a trade name of Everly Mews LLC.

  • Who receives them. Owners and staff of OpOrd clients, and the billing contacts of their vendors and suppliers, who have opted in.
  • How you opt in. By submitting the form on our text messages page with the consent box checked, or by telling us in writing that you agree to receive our billing texts. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Frequency. Message frequency varies with the billing cycle.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply. OpOrd does not charge for texts.
  • Opting out. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts; STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT also work. We’ll send one message confirming you’re unsubscribed. Reply START to opt back in.
