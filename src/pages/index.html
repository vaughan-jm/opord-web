The problem

The last week of every month.

1

Each GC wants its own pay-app form, its own waiver form, its own portal, and its own cutoff. Nothing is standard.

2

Every vendor and sub-sub on the job owes a waiver — the right type, the right through-date, the right amount — and one is always late.

3

When it is, the GC holds the whole draw. The work is done. The money waits another cycle. You float payroll.

What we take off your plate

The package, handled. The decisions, yours.

For the projects and GCs we agree on, every month:

  1. Know who owes what. We track which vendors and sub-subs owe documents for each project and pay period.
  2. Request early, on the right form. The GC’s waiver form, the correct through-date, the correct amount — sent at the start of the period, not the end.
  3. Follow up until it arrives. A defined cadence, then a clear escalation to your team for the vendors who need a phone call.
  4. Verify every returned document. Type, date, amount, and signature checked against what was requested — before it goes in the package.
  5. Assemble in each GC’s format. Pay app, waivers, and compliance documents in the order and form that GC’s portal expects.
  6. Hand you a signature-ready package. Before cutoff, with a short list of anything still outstanding and why.

You keep every decision. Nothing is signed, submitted, or waived without you.

How it works

Measure it. Fix one cycle. Then keep it running.

Step one

Billing Assessment

We map your month-end: GCs, forms, portals, vendors, and how often packages come back. You get a baseline and a straight recommendation — even if it’s “fix job costing first.”

Output: a baseline and a decision.

Step two

Waiver & Compliance Collection

We stand up collection and verification for one live billing cycle against an agreed target: every required document in hand a set number of days before cutoff.

Output: one clean cycle, on record.

Step three

Managed Month-End Billing

We run it every month, extend to full package assembly, and report against your baseline: days to package, kickbacks, and days to payment.

Output: month-end becomes a review, not a scramble.

Scope is set by active projects and GCs, with a defined exception allowance. Pricing follows the agreed scope. Where documents come back as scans or phone photos, we use software that reads them and flags anything it isn’t sure about for a person to check.

Questions

The things owners ask first.

Do you sign or submit anything for us?

No. We prepare the package and hand it to you ready for signature. You approve, sign, and submit — or authorize the upload after you’ve signed.

Do we need new software?

No. We work from your existing accounting or job-costing system and each GC’s forms and portals. If you already use a billing tool, we work alongside it.

Will you text our project managers and vendors?

Only people who have agreed to it. Your PMs and your vendors’ billing contacts opt in on our text messages page (or by texting START to our number), and anyone can reply STOP at any time. Anyone who hasn’t opted in hears from us another way, usually email.

Is this legal advice?

No. Lien rights, notice deadlines, and statutory waiver forms stay with your attorney. We prepare documents on the forms your GCs require; we don’t interpret lien law.

What does it cost?

Scope is set at the assessment, by active projects and GCs, and pricing follows that scope. We’re not publishing a rate card until we’ve run this with customers and can stand behind the numbers.

What happens when a vendor never responds?

We exhaust the agreed follow-up cadence, then hand you a specific exception — vendor, project, document, amount, attempts made — so your team can make one phone call instead of a week of them.

How do you handle our data?

Access is limited to named people, everything travels encrypted, bank access is read-only, and we never ask for your passwords. The details are on our security page.

Will this get us paid faster?

We don’t promise that, because GCs and owners control payment timing. What we commit to is the part that causes holds: complete packages, in the right format, before cutoff. We baseline your days-to-package, kickback rate, and days-to-payment at the start and report actuals against them.
